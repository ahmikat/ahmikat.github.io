Hello, I am Mikat.

I work on multimodal AI, connecting vision and language.

I am a senior CSE undergraduate at RUET, exploring multimodal AI, vision-language models, and NLP, especially for Bangla and other low-resource languages. I am also interested in efficient language models, data-efficient learning, and knowledge distillation.

Most of my time goes into research—reading papers, running experiments, and understanding what works and why, particularly when data and compute are limited.

Research interests

I am exploring how language and vision can work together, and how smaller models can learn effectively with limited data and compute. Bangla and other low-resource languages are a particular interest of mine.

01

Multimodal AI & Vision-Language Models

Connecting vision and language through multimodal representation learning, grounding, and evaluation.

02

Efficient NLP & Small Language Models

Compact language models, efficient pretraining, data-efficient learning, and training objectives such as MLM and RTD.

03

Knowledge Distillation & Teacher–Student Learning

Exploring how knowledge can be transferred from stronger models to smaller, more efficient models.

04

Low-Resource Language AI

NLP and multimodal research for Bangla and other languages with limited data and resources.

05

Document-Grounded Reasoning

Visual evidence localization, document understanding, and reasoning over complex documents.

Publications & research

Two accepted workshop papers at EMNLP 2026.

Publication 01 Accepted · Strict-Small Track BabyLM Workshop @ EMNLP 2026

Dense Supervision, Gentle Decay: Curriculum-Guided Rebalancing of ELECTRA-Style RTD and MLM for Small-Data Pretraining

Aftab Hossain Mikat, Tamjid Hasan Fahim

Studies curriculum-guided RTD–MLM rebalancing and training strategies for compact Bengali encoders under a strict data budget.

Preprint (PDF) ↗
Publication 02 Accepted · Archival workshop paper · Poster DocInsights Workshop @ EMNLP 2026 DocSem Shared Task

When the Evidence Is Right, the Math Is Easy: Understanding the Challenges of Document-Grounded Reasoning

Aftab Hossain Mikat, Tamjid Hasan Fahim

An analysis of document-grounded numerical reasoning, showing how visual evidence extraction and localization can be harder than the arithmetic itself.
