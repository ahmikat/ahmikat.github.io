Hello, I am Mikat.

I explore how compact models can learn from less.

I am a senior Computer Science & Engineering undergraduate at RUET, interested in two connected research directions: efficient Small Language Models and Multimodal AI that brings Natural Language Processing and Computer Vision together. I am particularly interested in building vision-language models for Bangla and other low-resource languages.

I enjoy moving between research and engineering—studying why a method works, then turning the idea into a reproducible system or a usable product.

02

Research interests

My work follows two equally important tracks: training capable Small Language Models efficiently, and building Multimodal AI systems that connect language with vision. A central goal is to make vision-language models practical for Bangla and other low-resource languages despite limited paired data and compute.

01

Small Language Models

Compact, capable models designed for constrained data and compute budgets.

02

Efficient Training

MLM, ELECTRA-style RTD, curriculum learning, and data-efficient supervision.

03

Multimodal AI: NLP + CV

Learning shared representations across language and vision through efficient multimodal systems.

04

Bangla Vision-Language Models

Exploring VLMs that connect Bangla text with visual information in low-resource settings.
